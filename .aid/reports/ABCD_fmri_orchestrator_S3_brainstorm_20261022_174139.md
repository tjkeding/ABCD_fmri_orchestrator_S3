    <topic id="H2" title="Rotation units of the producer confounds (revises O1)">
      <summary>The producer passes rotations through from the ABCD 3dvolreg motion TSV, documented as degrees (preproc.py 917-918), and computes framewise_displacement from the same array under the degree formula (970-973), so an FD cross-check cannot establish the physical unit (circular). Independent data check: ratio of rotational to translational FD components 0.166-1.053 (median 0.44) over 167 runs in three producer batches; radians misread as degrees would give about 0.005-0.01. Degrees confirmed. The max|rot| &lt;= 1.0 heuristic would flag 57% of runs.</summary>
      <decision status="decided" chosen="producer-consistency guard (option b)">(1) Treat rotations as degrees on provenance; document with the producer citation. (2) Per run, before the NSS trim, recompute Power FD (backward difference, rotations x pi/180 x 50 mm, frames 1..T-1) and compare with the producer framewise_displacement column using numpy.allclose(atol=1e-6 mm, rtol=0) (observed agreement 1.8e-15; a degree/radian change alters FD by about 0.01-1 mm). (3) Per-run QC field motion_units_check in {producer_fd_consistent, producer_fd_inconsistent, not_checkable}; on inconsistency exclude the run with reason producer_fd_inconsistent and classify that reason as EXIT_FAILED_UNEXPLAINED in classify_session_exit; no automatic conversion. Producer FD column absent or entirely non-finite: proceed with degrees, value not_checkable. (4) Remove the rotation_unit_ambiguous heuristic and its QC field. The unit-ratio pilot is complete and is not a separate test step.</decision>
